### The "Layers of Meta vanished" incident — root cause found

Xian sent his **real phone backup**. Traced the report ("I moved Layers
of Meta into a deck but its parent shows only 3 items and I can't find
it; I also can't reach crumlish.me"):

- Layers of Meta lives at DiP → online presence → **crumlish .me** →
  [deck created 2026-07-26T02:45] → Layers of Meta. That fresh deck is
  the signature of `moveCardInto` creating a deck on a target with none.
- **crumlish .me is `completed: true`.** So it moved active work *into a
  completed card*.
- The whole rest of the UI hides completed cards: `SubstackView` filters
  `!completed`, the sub-deck badge counts only unfinished. So crumlish.me
  (and everything inside it — Layers of Meta + its make-videos/karaoke
  subtree) fell off every navigable surface. online presence's badge = 3
  (the three *unfinished* siblings), exactly what Xian saw.
- The **MoveIntoPicker walked the whole tree with NO completion filter**,
  so it offered a done card as a target. That was the hole.

### The invariant, now enforced: a completed card is sealed

Xian's directive: *"we can't allow cards to be added to finished cards or
decks, clearly."* Enforced end-to-end (test-first, RED ZONE):

1. **MoveIntoPicker** now uses new domain helper `reachableMoveTargets` —
   only ACTIVE cards, descending only through active cards (you can only
   move a card somewhere you could *navigate* to). Skips completed cards
   and their whole subtree.
2. **`moveCardInto`** rejects a completed target (defense in depth).
3. **`addSubstackTask`** rejects when the owning card is completed;
   **`createSubstack`** rejects adding a deck to a completed card
   (new helper `findCardOwningDeck`).
4. **Completion guard deepened**: the R1.1b "can't complete a card with
   unfinished interior" block only checked DIRECT children — a card whose
   direct kids were all done but had unfinished *grandchildren* completed
   anyway, burying them. Now uses `unfinishedDescendants` (whole subtree,
   including work stranded under an already-done intermediate). Also fixed
   a latent crash: `blockingDeck` used a `!` assertion that would throw
   when the blocker was deeper than direct children; now optional.

86 tests green (was 78), `tsc -p tsconfig.app.json` clean.
