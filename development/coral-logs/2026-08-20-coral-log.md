## Session #3: shake-undo root cause found — precise repro cracked it

Xian's repro was exact and specific enough to solve it on inspection:
create a card, complete it, shake, an **"Undo Typing" / "Redo Typing"**
dialog appears, tapping Undo does nothing. That title was the
tell — checked `en.json`'s own `undoDialog` strings (`"Undo last
action?"` / `"Undo"`) and confirmed they don't match at all. What he's
tapping is iOS's own native shake-to-undo-text-entry system feature
(same one in Notes/Mail), not our `ActionSheet` component. Mechanism:
iOS's OS-level gesture recognizer catches the physical shake before our
`devicemotion` listener does, shows its own alert with nothing live
left to undo (hence the silent no-op), and likely prevents our own
sheet from ever opening — no code bug, a platform collision. This isn't
guessable from source inspection alone (I tried last message and
correctly found nothing, since there was nothing to find in our code)
— needed his exact repro to crack.

This **fully resolves rollup item 12**, open since 2026-07-29 ("ships
blind until a device confirms it") — a device confirmed it, with a
real mechanism, four weeks later. Rewrote item 12 to close it and point
at item 24's fuller writeup rather than leaving two overlapping open
items.
