### 1 · Usage metrics leaking into a store screenshot (rc.36)
Chasing an unexplained byte-diff on `ipad-13/06` — a file nothing I had
changed should have touched — found the Settings shot rendering R1.5's
"Your usage" panel with counts generated by the capture automation:
*Cards created: 0 · Cards deferred: 1 · Deepest deferral: 1 passes on
one card.* Non-deterministic, meaningless to a stranger, and sitting
directly beneath a caption reading "No account, no cloud, no tracking."

Visible only on iPad — the taller viewport reaches the panel — so a
phone-only check could never have found it. Fixed by clearing
`oneJobMetrics` immediately before shot 06, so the store shows what a
genuinely new user sees.

**Prediction I got wrong, recorded because it mattered:** I told Xian
the panel was "cut off below the fold" on iPhone. The monitor then

never below the fold — it was *displacing the Backup section out of

### 2 · Sub-deck view collapsed instead of filling the screen (rc.37)
Xian, reviewing the set: *"some are top aligned with the (+) floating
high and some are bottom aligned… maybe we need center-alignment in the
app?"* He offered it as a design choice. It was a bug, and the app
already wanted what he asked for.

The sub-deck branch wraps `SubstackView` in a `motion.div` (slide-in
animation) carrying **no flex classes**. As a flex child it defaults to
sizing to its content, so `SubstackView`'s own `flex-1` and
`TaskStack`'s `items-center justify-center` had nothing to fill or
centre against. The whole view collapsed to content height and sat at
the top. The main deck has no such wrapper and was centring correctly
all along.

One line (`flex flex-col flex-1 min-h-0`). Every view now centres its
card with "+" pinned at the bottom — covenant 1 says a card is a card
at every depth, and the layout finally says so too. This also **retires
the "sub-deck top-aligns" note I had filed as a post-1.0 design
question**: it was never a decision anyone needed to make.

Set re-shot against rc.37 → `staging-2026-09-05`; 09-04 retired so only
one candidate folder exists; walkthrough and rollup paths updated.

## Design discussion: R2.2b, wide-viewport peeks
His proposal from the iPad shots: on viewports that float in horizontal
space, continue the peek out to a full card outline with a gradient
fade "so nothing further is expected past it."

Grounded it in the code before responding: `CanvasPeek` pins a 14px
sliver to the CONTAINER edge (`max-w-md mx-auto`). On a phone that edge
IS the screen edge, so the sliver works by implication — clipped,
therefore continuing. On iPad nothing clips it and the implication
collapses.

Argued his version over the cheaper alternative (pin to the viewport
edge instead), because on a wide canvas the truthful statement is
"there is another deck, over there," which a card shape can say and a
distant sliver cannot. Added two constraints: outline-and-hue only,
never content (content would make it the postponed R2 canvas view
arriving disguised as polish); and the fade must *terminate* rather
than soften, since a hard edge implies another card past it. Raised one
question I can't answer: whether a full outline still reads as *the
afterlife* on the grey variant without a label. Recorded as R2.2b with
all of it.
