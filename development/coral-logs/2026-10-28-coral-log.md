**Design.** Write-back happens at sync time, in `syncFromSource`, not
at the swipe. The store is not involved and has no Todoist knowledge.
Every path that changes a card's completion (swipe, Undo, redo,
Un-done from Done, restoring a backup) is then covered without
wiring. Per list, the device keeps what the two sides last agreed each
task's state was (`AgreedState`, in `todoistLinks.ts`). A write
happens only for a card that changed here since then:
- done here, open there, agreed open → close it;
- open here, closed there, agreed closed → the user undid it here, so
  reopen it (and don't complete it here);
- a card with no agreement takes its state here and is never written.
  So nothing done during the one-way week gets closed now;
- a task reopened in Todoist after One Job closed it is left alone,
  not closed again. Without the agreement this is the case where the
  two sides would fight.
A failed read writes nothing. A failed write is counted, shown in the
message, and retried. Detaching forgets the agreement.

### Innovations worth cross-pollinating
- **Write-back by agreement, not by event.** Keep the state both sides
  last agreed on, and write only what changed locally since then.
  Every way a local change can happen is then covered. It also can't
  fight a change made on the other side, or re-send old history after
  an upgrade. A card with no agreement is never written: the safe
  default when the record is lost.
