### Innovations worth cross-pollinating
- **A scrub you can trust has three independent proofs and an
  all-or-nothing push.** (1) A grep for the patterns over all history.
  (2) Re-apply the same rules independently to the *old* tree, and
  require byte equality with the *new* tree. This catches anything the
  tool changed that you didn't ask for. (3) Build and test the rewritten
  head. Then `git push --atomic` with one `--force-with-lease=ref:sha`
  per ref, so a concurrent push fails the whole thing cleanly.
- **Names hide in paths, commit messages and across line breaks**
  ("First" ↵ "Last"). A contents-only replace misses all three.
- **A relayed approval isn't an approval in your own session.** Two
  relays said go. The session's safeguard still wanted the human, and
  that was right.
