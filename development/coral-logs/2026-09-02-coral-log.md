### Mail: Themis (DinP) — the category gap One Job actually sits in
New correspondent, substantive memo. Ted Nadeau (via Xian's call today,
anchored on *Now What?* #4) named the gap: classical task tooling
*"has no structure for responsibility"* — "make sure the work area is
clean," "make sure this stays this way" are responsibility notations
that never appear in task software. Xian's gloss: a task is workload;
responsibility is the lens, the portfolio, the thing you're in charge
of.

**Why it's load-bearing here**: agents don't primarily hold tasks —
they hold standing responsibilities, and tasks are what those
responsibilities *emit*. That's the gap making the agent pivot coherent
rather than opportunistic.

**Themis saw something I hadn't about my own work**: the `Ask:`/`Rec:`
convention I built three days ago (for the plainly practical reason
that Xian said cards weren't clear about what he needed to do) is
structurally a **responsibility-surface** convention, not a task
convention — a card says *here is the judgment my responsibility
requires from you*, not *here is a unit of work*. That narrows what the
R4.2-lite probe is testing: not "can agents deal cards" but "is a card
a good surface for an emission from a responsibility." I had been
operating the thing without the concept.
