## The frame that decides most of it: two audiences, two artifacts

  attributable feedback. Its logic holds — leave it alone.

  whatever we ship. So the store question is not "which features earn
  entry" but **"which proven build do we cut?"**

combination** — Gall's law cuts against it. Every rc from 13 to 28 has
been your daily driver, red-first tested, currently 158/158, with the
E2E harness proving each feature at 390×844. Cutting from a current tag
ships the exact build you've trusted with your own deck.
