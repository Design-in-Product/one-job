copy, privacy, and review info all carry over untouched.

## Pre-submit check (2 min, on your phone)
Build 39 from TestFlight: with a deck going, add a card via Shortcuts,
open One Job. Your top card should be unchanged with the new card
right behind it. That device-confirms the one behavior 1.1's notes
promise that hasn't been device-tested.

1. **My Apps → One Job → ⊕ next to "iOS App"** → new version → **1.1**.
2. **What's New** (paste):

   > One Job now works with Apple Shortcuts. A new "Add Card" action
   > lets Shortcuts and Siri add cards to your deck: pipe in Reminders,
   > build importers, or add cards by voice. Cards added from outside
   > join the deck behind your current card, because the card you're
   > on is the one that matters.

   > safety.

3. **Build** → select **1.1 (39)**.
4. Everything else (screenshots, description, review notes, privacy):
   unchanged — leave as is.
5. **Manual release** again recommended (you time the Teresa ping).
6. Add for Review → Submit.
