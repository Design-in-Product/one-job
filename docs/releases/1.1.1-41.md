# 1.1.1 (41) — Siri cards show up while the app is open

**Follows 40** (2026-10-06). It fixes the two things xian found testing 40.

## What's new in 41

**A card you add with Siri while One Job is open now appears right
away.** Before, Siri's window covered the app without the app ever
leaving the screen, so the new card waited until you quit and
reopened. Now One Job checks for new cards when Siri (or Control
Center, or a notification) gets out of the way, and the deck on screen
refreshes as soon as they arrive.

**The card goes into the deck you're looking at.** In 40, cards added
with the app open went into whichever deck you happened to be on at
the next restart, because that's when they were finally picked up. Now
they're picked up straight away, so they land in the deck in front of
you.

Everything from 40 is still here: Siri cards in sentence case, and the
safer backup restore.

## What to test

1. With One Job open on a deck, say "Add a card to One Job" and give a
   title. When Siri closes, the card should be right behind your top
   card. No restart.
2. Switch to a different deck and do it again. The card should land in
   that deck.
3. With One Job closed, add a card with Siri, then open the app. The
   card should appear in the deck the app opens to (as before).
