# 1.1.1 (40) — Siri cards come out in sentence case

**Follows 39** (2026-09-12). Held from 09-17 on purpose. xian said
"ship 40" on 2026-10-05, via Janus. It's a new version number because
1.1 is live on the App Store and its version line is closed.

## What's new in 40

**Cards you add with Siri or Shortcuts now come out in sentence
case.** Before, "call the dentist" could arrive as "Call The Dentist".
The fix tells the system the field takes a sentence, so dictation
capitalises the first word and proper names on its own: "Call Maria Lopez"
stays right. The app doesn't rewrite what you said afterwards.

**Restoring a backup is harder to get wrong.**
- A backup file with no cards in it is refused rather than allowed to
  wipe a full deck.
- Cards go back into the right decks even if you've renamed a deck
  since the backup.
- An older build can no longer overwrite data saved by a newer one.

## What to test

1. **Siri, two cards:** one short ("Add a card to One Job: call the
   dentist") and one full sentence. Each should come out in sentence
   case, with any names still capitalised.
2. Shortcuts' Add Card action: the same check, typed instead of spoken.
3. If you're comfortable with it: Settings → export a backup, open the
   file, then import it back. Your decks and cards should be unchanged.
