# 1.1.1 (43) — the open card is a card

**Follows 42** (2026-10-12), which was not tested on its own. 43
carries everything in 42 plus the rebuilt open card.

## What's new in 43

**Tap a card and it opens as a card.** It keeps the deck card's
shape, a little larger. Status, Created, the Description heading and
the Edit and Close buttons are gone.

**Tap the title or the description to edit it.** There is no Edit
button. Your change is saved when you leave the field or close the
card. A title left blank goes back to what it was.

**The deck inside a card is much bigger** and wears the colour of the
deck it belongs to.

## From 42

**A card you add with Siri or a Shortcut lands on top of the deck,**
exactly like a card you type in. Siri no longer says the card will
appear "next time you open".

## What to test

1. Tap the top card. It should open as a card, with no Status or
   Created line and no Edit button.
2. Tap the title, change it, then tap the description. The title
   should be saved. Change the description and close the card; open
   it again and the description should be saved.
3. Open a card that holds a deck. The deck should be large and in the
   colour of the deck you are in. Tap it; it should open.
4. With One Job open, say "Add a card to One Job" and give a title.
   The new card should be the top card, and Siri should say "Added to
   your One Job deck".
5. With One Job closed, add two cards with Siri, then open the app.
   The second should be on top, with the first right behind it.

## Not in this build

Cards inside a card's own deck still can't be edited.
