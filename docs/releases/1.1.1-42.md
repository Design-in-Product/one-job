# 1.1.1 (42) — a Siri card lands on top

**Follows 41** (2026-10-08). It changes the two things xian found
testing 41.

## What's new in 42

**A card you add with Siri or a Shortcut now lands on top of the
deck,** exactly like a card you type in. Before, it slid in behind
your top card, which left it hidden. If you add several before
opening the app, the last one you said is on top.

**Siri no longer says the card will appear "next time you open".**
Since 41 it appears at once, and the Add Card action's description
and Siri's reply now say so.

Everything from 41 is still here: the card appears straight away with
the app open, in the deck you're looking at.

## What to test

1. With One Job open on a deck, say "Add a card to One Job" and give a
   title. When Siri closes, the new card should be the top card. No
   restart.
2. Listen to Siri's reply. It should say "Added to your One Job deck",
   with nothing about opening the app.
3. With One Job closed, add two cards with Siri, then open the app.
   The second one should be on top, with the first right behind it.
