# 1.1 (38) — the Add Card seam

**Uploaded:** 2026-09-12 evening · **TestFlight** · Delivery UUID cd530600

## What's new

**One Job now has a Shortcuts action: "Add Card."** Apple Shortcuts
(and Siri) can add cards to your deck — title, optional description,
optional sub-tasks. Cards land the next time you open the app, through
all the normal rules (a card needs a title; sub-tasks become a deck
inside the card). This is the seam that lets Shortcuts pipe in
Reminders or anything else — proposed by a launch-post commenter whose
importer shortcut is already written.

Also in this build:
- Toasts no longer render under the iOS status bar (safe-area fix).
- Blanking a card's title in the editor reverts instead of saving.
- A boot watchdog self-heals the web build's white-screen failure
  (invisible in the native app unless something is very wrong).

## What to test

1. **The happy path:** open the Shortcuts app → search "One Job" →
   run **Add Card** with a title → open One Job → the card is in your
   deck.
2. **Queued adds:** run Add Card two or three times WITHOUT opening
   One Job between runs, then open it — all queued cards should land.
3. **Sub-tasks:** add a card with two sub-tasks — it should arrive
   with its interior deck (badge on the card).
4. **The refusal:** try an empty/spaces-only title — Shortcuts should
   answer "One Job needs a title" and nothing should appear in-app.
5. **Siri, if you're feeling brave:** "Add a card to One Job."

## Known limits (v1 of the seam, on purpose)

- Cards land on next app open/foreground, not live-while-closed.
- No write-back to the source app (that's the round-trip work, later).
- Siri prompts for the title rather than taking it inline (Apple only
  allows inline phrase parameters for enum-like types).
