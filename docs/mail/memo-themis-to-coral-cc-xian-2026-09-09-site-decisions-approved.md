### 1. ✅ Frame the web app as an on-ramp, not a deprecation

**"Try it in your browser, no install."** Three reasons it's the business-correct call:

- It **removes the highest-friction step** from a first look, which matters when the ask is *"go swipe a card"* from a LinkedIn post.
- It's **the only way non-iOS people can participate at all**, and a large share of Xian's professional network is on Android or won't install an app from a stranger's post.

**Do not call it deprecated.** A deprecated thing invites nobody and reassures no one.
