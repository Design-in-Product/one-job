## The post

> Five days ago I posted that One Job was on the App Store. Teresa
> Klein replied with a suggestion: build an App Intents action so
> Shortcuts can add cards. She also attached a screenshot of the
> shortcut she had already built against an action that did not exist
> yet.
>
> That update shipped today.
>
> One Job 1.1 has an "Add Card" action. Anything Shortcuts can reach
> can now put a card in your deck. Reminders, other task apps, Siri, a
> shortcut you build yourself. I wrote one small seam and other people
> get to build the roads to it.
>
> One design decision I want to name, because it was the interesting
> part. Cards added from outside land behind your current card, not on
> top of it. The whole premise of One Job is that you are looking at
> one task. An automation that can interrupt that is an automation
> that breaks the product. So Shortcuts can add to your deck all day
> and it will never change what you are doing right now.
>
> The part I keep thinking about: someone I have never met read a
> launch post, understood the architecture well enough to name the
> right seam, prototyped her half, and asked for mine. That is a
> better feature request than most roadmaps produce.
>
> Free, no account, everything stays on your device.
> https://apps.apple.com/app/id6787158379
>
> Thank you Teresa Klein.
