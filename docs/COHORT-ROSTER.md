# Cohort roster — raised hands and their clocks

**Maintained by:** Coral · started 2026-09-15 at Xian's request.
**Why it exists:** rolling starts mean every participant has their own
day-0. Activation (5 cards + 2 active days) and retention (4 active
days in days 22–28) are measured per person, so "when did this one
start" is the only number that makes their data readable. A shared
cohort date would have made a spreadsheet unnecessary; rolling starts
make it mandatory.

**Privacy posture:** names and where they raised a hand, nothing more.
No emails (the TestFlight public link is self-serve — see below), no
notes about anyone's work or setup beyond what they volunteered in
public. If someone shares a usage summary, it goes in their own file,
not here.

## The weekly question

One question a week, drafted by Coral, sent by Xian. The bank lives in
`docs/COHORT-CHECKIN-QUESTIONS.md`. **Week 1 is the disambiguator** —
"is pushing a card usually 'not today' or 'this comes back every
week'?" — because the deferral metric cannot separate intent from
friction on its own, and every later number is read differently
depending on the answer.

## Running the rolling clock

- **Week-4 dates are computed from day-0, not from joining.** Someone
  can install and not start for a week; their trial begins when they
  use it.
- **The weekly check-in is one question**, per the deets: not a survey,
  no homework. Coral drafts the question; Xian sends it.
- **Day-0 is observable only if they tell us or share a usage
  summary.** Absent that, use "first week they report anything" and
  mark it derived, not observed. (Same provenance discipline as
  `firstUseSource` in the metrics store: a guessed date must never be
  mistaken for a known one.)

## What this roster is not

Not a CRM, not a funnel, not a place for anyone's private information.
Ten people is small enough that attention beats automation — the
instrument here is a table and a human reading it.
